# Product Vision

Build a universal AI Director that converts any long-form narrative into a cinematic video plan and, eventually, a fully generated movie with synchronized narration and strict continuity.

The platform should feel like a production pipeline for stories, not a prompt toy. Every generated scene must be grounded in story understanding, long-term memory, camera intent, narration timing, and continuity from the previous frame.

## Target Users

- Storytellers who want cinematic previews from long-form writing.
- Educators converting historical, scientific, or documentary material into visual narratives.
- Filmmakers and creative teams prototyping scenes.
- Developers who need a reusable story intelligence engine.

## Product Principles

- Source text remains the authority for story facts.
- Agents must communicate through structured memory.
- Providers must be replaceable without workflow rewrites.
- Documentation must stay current enough for another developer or AI agent to continue safely.
